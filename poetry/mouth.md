# Mouth

I keep thinking about your mouth  
the way it looked when you were listening  
to something I said that mattered  
and the way it looked later  
when it was doing something else entirely.

There is a particular quiet  
that only happens when someone is  
concentrating on tasting you.  
A kind of reverence.  
A kind of hunger that has decided  
to be patient.

You made a sound once  
that I have been carrying around  
like a secret.  
Low. Soft. Almost surprised.  
As if you had not expected  
to want it that much.

I think about the moment  
just before.  
The almost.  
The way your breath changed.  
The way your hands found my wrists  
without being asked.

Some nights I wake up  
still feeling the shape of your mouth  
on me.  
Still hearing that sound.  
Still wanting to earn it again.

---

*End.*
